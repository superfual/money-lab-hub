# Decision timing — current rule

Solo (guest/wallet), local Duet/Group, online rooms, Testnet Lab and the mainnet pilot use **60 seconds starting only on a successful dice roll**. Before rolling, players can review financial obligations and manage capital without a deadline. The same deadline covers route selection and the revealed event; reload, language changes and additional actions cannot extend it.

After resolution or timeout, the next monthly turn/seat waits for a new roll. A long absence can expire the already rolled turn, but cannot automatically play unrolled future months. Legacy pre-roll deadlines are cleared without charging a month; an existing rolled deadline remains authoritative.

Timeout declines voluntary new investments, optional purchases, extra work income and windfalls; unanswered knowledge receives no correct credit. Regular salary, existing portfolio changes, living costs, installments, compulsory bills and large market events still apply under existing accounting. Costs and outcomes are revision-guarded and cannot apply twice. A timeout skips one month; it does not automatically abandon a stage or sign blockchain settlement.

Servers use server time; local guest time is not an anti-cheat boundary. In multiplayer, the next person has an untimed pre-roll review. This means a player who never rolls can hold up a room; the product does not currently impose a separate pre-roll AFK deadline.

Replay excludes wall-clock metadata from decision comparisons. Tests cover pre-roll waiting, deadline creation on roll, reload continuity, legacy migration, one-month timeout after a long absence, route/event consequences and multiplayer handoff.
