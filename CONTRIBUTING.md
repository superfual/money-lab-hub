# Build Money Lab with us

Money Lab is currently a solo-founder, AI-assisted project. Fual Nguyễn is actively looking for teammates to refine World 1 and collaboratively develop World 2 and World 3.

## Where help matters

- **World 1:** educational content, playtesting, balancing, accessibility, mobile UX and visual design.
- **Worlds 2/3:** investment-world rules, multiplayer architecture, property/construction/operating cash flows and progression.
- **Web3 engineering:** review of circuit integration, server-attestation boundaries, contract testing and future gas sponsorship.
- **Education and product:** financial-literacy curriculum, localization, player research and sustainable business-model experiments.

Worlds 2/3 already have first playable prototypes. Their long-term mechanics, content and production quality are open for collaborative development. Future token utility and gas sponsorship are research directions, not existing features or promises of financial returns.

Open a GitHub issue titled **[Team] Your area of interest**, describe your relevant experience and the part you would like to help with. Do not post private keys, credentials or personal financial records. This invitation does not promise compensation, equity or token allocation; any working arrangement would be agreed separately.

For code changes, open a focused PR with the problem, proposed behavior and validation. Run `npm ci`, `npm test`, and `npm run build`. Keep educational USD, ordinary HUB, testnet points and mainnet pilot points distinct. Never include runtime secrets, database snapshots or wallet private keys. Never present offchain logic or a server signature as fully trustless computation.
